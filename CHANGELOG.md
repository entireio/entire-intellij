# Changelog

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-27)

The first release on [JetBrains Marketplace](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/36279-entire), for JetBrains IDEs 2025.3.1 through 2026.3.

- The Checkpoints and Sessions tabs list the checkpoints behind the current branch's commits and the agent sessions behind them. A click opens a checkpoint or a session, with its transcript, in an editor tab.
- The Trails tab lists the trails of a repository on entire.com. The trail panel shows a trail's changes, merge gates, CI runs, and timeline.
- The Brain tab shows what the entire-brain plugin reports about the repository's brain, and View Brain opens its graph.
- Each tab's search field filters the rows it has loaded, and Enter runs Entire's search for commits, checkpoints, and sessions on every branch.
- The Setup tab installs the Entire CLI, logs in, enables Entire, and builds the graph and the brain, one step at a time. Each step shows its command and runs only when you click it.
